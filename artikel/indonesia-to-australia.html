What Can You Send to Australia?

Permissible items must comply with Indonesian export regulations and strict Australian Customs (ABF) and Biosecurity (DAFF) rules. Australia has very strict quarantine laws. Common items shipped include:

  • Apparel, textiles, and fashion accessories (Batik, Hijab)
  • Handicrafts and handmade goods
  • Official documents and paperwork for students / visa
  • Personal effects for Indonesian students & workers in Australia
  • Local UMKM brand products
  • Pre-packaged dry foods and snacks (must have full ingredient label, factory sealed)
  • Commercial goods for B2B transactions

Note: Australia strictly prohibits fresh foods, seeds, wooden items without fumigation, liquids, and products containing pork. All food must be commercially packaged. Please check with our team beforehand regarding item admissibility to avoid biosecurity fines up to AUD 5,000.

How Are Shipping Rates to Australia Calculated?

International shipping fees from Bandung to Australia depend on several parameters:

  • Actual Weight vs. Volumetric Weight: Charged based on whichever value is higher. Formula: (L x W x H in cm) / 5000
  • Destination Address: Direct door-to-door delivery across all areas in Australia (e.g., Sydney NSW, Melbourne VIC, Brisbane QLD, Perth WA, Adelaide SA, etc.).
  • Service Type: The speed and tier of international delivery chosen (Express 3-5 days).
  • Commodity Category: Standard items or specialized shipments.
