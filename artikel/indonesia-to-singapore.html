What Can You Send to Singapore?

Permissible items must comply with Indonesian export regulations and Singapore Customs (ICA & SFA) rules. Common items shipped include:

  • Apparel, textiles, and fashion accessories
  • Handicrafts and handmade goods
  • Official documents and paperwork
  • Personal effects and household items
  • Local UMKM brand products
  • Pre-packaged dry foods and snacks (compliant with Singapore Food Agency regulations)
  • Commercial goods for B2B transactions

Note: Liquids, hazardous chemicals, and dangerous goods (DG) require special handling or may be restricted. Please check with our team beforehand regarding item admissibility.

How Are Shipping Rates to Singapore Calculated?

International shipping fees from Bandung to Singapore depend on several parameters:

  • Actual Weight vs. Volumetric Weight: Charged based on whichever value is higher. Formula: (L x W x H in cm) / 5000
  • Destination Address: Direct door-to-door delivery across all areas in Singapore (e.g., Jurong, Tampines, Orchard, Woodlands, etc.).
  • Service Type: The speed and tier of international delivery chosen.
  • Commodity Category: Standard items or specialized shipments.
