What Should You Know Before Shipping to Malaysia?

International shipping involves more than transporting a parcel from one location to another. Shipments may need to comply with customs regulations, import requirements, and the carrier's shipping policies.

  • Check the contents: Provide an accurate description of the items you intend to ship so their eligibility can be checked.
  • Measure the parcel: Prepare the parcel's weight and dimensions to help determine the applicable shipping charges.
  • Provide the correct address: Include the recipient's full name, street address, city, postcode, and active contact number.
  • Prepare the necessary documents: Some shipments may require additional information or supporting documents for customs clearance.

What Items Can Be Shipped to Malaysia?

The items accepted for international shipping depend on Malaysian import regulations, customs requirements, and the carrier's service conditions. Not every item can be shipped internationally.

Before sending clothing, gifts, food products, cosmetics, samples, or commercial goods, confirm whether the items are eligible for shipment. Certain products may require permits, additional documentation, or special handling.

Dangerous goods, prohibited substances, restricted products, and other regulated items must comply with applicable laws and carrier policies. Always check the requirements before arranging your shipment.

How Long Does Shipping to Malaysia Take?

Delivery times depend on the selected shipping service, the destination, transportation schedules, and customs clearance procedures. Additional inspections or incomplete documentation may also affect the delivery timeframe.

If your parcel is needed by a specific date, ask for the current estimated transit time before arranging the shipment. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed unless the applicable service terms explicitly provide a guarantee.

How to Prepare a Parcel for International Shipping

Proper preparation can help reduce avoidable shipping problems. Consider the following steps before sending your parcel to Malaysia.

  1. Confirm that the contents are permitted for international shipping.
  2. Use suitable packaging and protective materials to help prevent damage during transportation.
  3. Write the recipient's full address and contact details clearly and accurately.
  4. Provide truthful information about the contents and value of the goods when required for customs documentation.
  5. Check the shipping charges, estimated delivery time, and required documents before confirming the shipment.
  6. Keep the tracking number so you can monitor the parcel's progress.
