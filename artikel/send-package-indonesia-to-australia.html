2. How Much Does It Cost to Send a Package from Indonesia to Australia?

Shipping cost to Australia is calculated based on actual weight or volumetric weight — whichever is higher. The formula is: (Length x Width x Height in cm) / 5000 = volumetric kg.

Example: A box 40 x 30 x 20 cm = 24,000 / 5000 = 4.8 kg volumetric. If your actual weight is only 3 kg, you will be charged for 4.8 kg. So pack as compact as possible!

  • Documents (under 0.5 kg): Cheapest, ideal for certificates, passports, university documents
  • Small parcel 1 - 5 kg: Most popular for snacks, clothes, hijab, skincare, gifts — best value for students and families
  • Bulk 10 - 30 kg: Better rate per kg, ideal for online sellers shipping to Australia
  • Cargo 30 kg+: Ask for special cargo rate, usually cheaper for business shipments

3. What Can You Send to Australia? And What Is Prohibited?

Australia has one of the strictest biosecurity rules in the world to protect its unique environment. The Australian Border Force (ABF) and Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry check every parcel. If you send a prohibited item, your parcel can be seized, destroyed, or returned — and you can be fined.

✅ SAFE TO SEND to Australia:

  • Clothes, hijab, batik, shoes, bags
  • Books, documents, printed materials
  • Skincare, cosmetics (sealed, non-aerosol, with ingredients list)
  • Electronics, phone accessories, cables (with battery declaration if needed)
  • Factory-sealed dry snacks, instant noodles, coffee, tea (commercial packaging, check ingredients)
  • Handicrafts, souvenirs (no untreated wood, no seeds, no soil)

❌ PROHIBITED / HIGH RISK - DO NOT SEND:

  • Fresh food, meat, eggs, dairy, honey: Fresh fruit, vegetables, uncanned meat, milk, cheese, honey and bee products are generally prohibited
  • Live plants, seeds, soil, sand: Strict biosecurity ban
  • Tobacco, cigarettes, vape: Prohibited, except cigars with special rules
  • Drugs, medicines without permit: Prescription medicines need Australian Department of Health permit
  • Weapons, replica firearms, knives, pepper spray: Prohibited or need police certification
  • Cash, gold, jewellery with ivory, counterfeit goods: Prohibited or need special permit
  • Used bedding, untreated wood, straw packing: Prohibited as packing material

Important: Do NOT use straw, dried leaves, or fruit cartons as packing. Use bubble wrap and new cardboard box. Declare everything honestly on the customs form. A false declaration is worse than a high tax.

4. Australian Customs, Duty & GST - What Will the Receiver Pay?

For most personal parcels, Australia applies:

  • Import Duty: 0% - 5% for most goods, depending on HS Code
  • GST 10%: Goods and Services Tax applies on most imports, including low-value imports under AUD 1,000 for online purchases
  • Customs Declaration: CN22 for under 2 kg, CN23 for heavier parcels — we help you fill it
  • IA-CEPA: Indonesia-Australia Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (since July 2020) can reduce duty for eligible goods — ask us if your product qualifies

For gifts under AUD 1,000, the receiver usually pays little or no duty, but GST may still apply depending on the seller / platform. Always tell the receiver to keep the tracking number and be ready to show ID if Australian customs asks.

5. How to Pack Your Parcel for Australia - Step by Step

  1. Use a strong new box: Double-wall box for over 5 kg. Do not reuse fruit cartons.
  2. Wrap each item: Bubble wrap for fragile items, plastic bag for liquids.
  3. Fill empty space: No movement inside the box. Shake test: if it rattles, add more filler.
  4. Seal with H-tape: Tape all edges, not just the middle.
  5. Label clearly: Full name, full address, postcode, phone number in Australia + sender details in Indonesia.
  6. Declare honestly: Item name, quantity, value in USD / AUD. Example: “2 pcs batik shirt - USD 30 - Gift”
